# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Switchboard is for people trying to make social plans without turning every invitation into a high-pressure group thread. Users are usually on mobile, moving quickly, and deciding who to ask, how to coordinate, and how much social context to reveal.

## Product Purpose

Switchboard removes friction from making plans: cascading invites, private group decisions, mutual-interest matching, availability signals, and focused plan rooms. Success means more plans happen with less awkwardness, fewer broadcasts, and clearer consent.

## Brand Personality

Warm, direct, quietly playful. The product should feel social and human, but still trustworthy enough for private contacts, invitations, and relationship signals.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate scheduling tools, overbuilt CRM-style contact managers, generic social feeds, and manipulative growth loops. The app should not pressure users to spam their address book or expose private interest before both sides have consented.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next social action obvious.
2. Preserve privacy by default, reveal only what the user expects.
3. Keep invite and friend flows fast enough for mobile use.
4. Prefer clear status feedback over silent loading or hidden failure.
5. Let playful moments support the task, not distract from it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast and keyboard operability. Respect reduced motion. Contact and invite flows must communicate results in text, not color alone, and must provide non-contact-picker fallbacks for browsers that do not support contact access.
